HOW TO PLAY

You and the dealer each get seven cards from a 53-card deck (52 cards and a Joker). You split yours into a five-card high hand and a two-card low hand. In Face Up Pai Gow the dealer's seven cards are dealt face up and set the House Way before you set yours, so you know exactly what you're playing against.

1 · Bet

Tap a chip, then BET ($5–$500). The Bonus and Ace High (up to $100 each) and the $5 jackpot light are optional. Then tap Deal; at a friends' table, Deal me in.

2 · Set your hand

Tap two cards for your low hand, then Set hand. Your five-card hand must beat your two-card hand. Or tap Help and your best hand against the dealer's is set for you straight away. At a friends' table you have 45 seconds; then your hand is set for you.

The Joker counts as an Ace, or completes a straight, a flush or a straight flush. A-2-3-4-5 is the second-best straight. Five Aces (four Aces and the Joker) is the best hand.

3 · Showdown

  • Win both hands: you win 1 to 1. No commission.
  • Win one, lose one: push. Lose both: you lose. A tie on a hand (a copy) goes to the dealer.
  • When the dealer's hand is an Ace-high pai gow (no pair, Ace high), every main bet pushes.
