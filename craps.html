HOW TO PLAY CRAPS

Throwing the dice

With 3D throw on, tapping Throw the dice takes you to the rail of a 3D table: pick where to stand, tap a die to set it, then flick the dice to the far end. Everyone else's throws show there too.

When it's your roll, tap a die to set the face you want on top, then flick the dice up the table (or tap Throw the dice). They bounce off the back wall like a real throw. Setting the dice is part of the fun: every throw is still random. At a friends' table the shooter has 45 seconds; after a seven-out the dice pass to the next player.

Regular craps

  • Come-out roll: Pass Line wins on 7 or 11, loses on 2, 3 or 12. Any other number becomes the point (the ON puck).
  • Point: Pass Line wins if the point rolls before a 7. A 7 first is a seven-out.
  • Don't Pass is the opposite: wins on 2 or 3, pushes on 12, loses on 7 or 11; then wins if a 7 comes before the point.
  • Come / Don't Come work the same way, starting from the next roll. Tap your Come bet on its number to add odds.

Crapless craps

There's no craps on the come-out: only a 7 wins, and 2, 3, 11 and 12 become points just like 4 to 10. There's no Don't Pass or Don't Come, and you can place or buy 2, 3, 11 and 12 too.

Limits

Pick your table with Table limits, like the baccarat rooms. Numbers and the line: Lounge $10–$5,000 · High Limit $25–$50,000 · VIP Salon $100–$500,000 · Private Room $1,000–$5,000,000 · Diamond Room $10,000–$1,000,000,000,000. Tap again to press (add more). Side bets (props, hardways and the bonus) are $10 minimum at the Lounge, $25 at High Limit and $50 from the VIP Salon up; the bonus has no maximum, and odds go up to 3-4-5× your line bet.

Place and buy bets (tap a number)

Place bets pay house odds. Buy bets pay true odds with no commission. Use the Numbers: PLACE / BUY button to switch. Both stay up when they win, are off on the come-out roll, and lose on a 7.

Odds (behind the line)

Pass and Come odds pay true odds, up to 3× on 4 and 10, 4× on 5 and 9, 5× on 6 and 8 (3× on 2, 3, 11 and 12 at crapless). Lay odds behind Don't Pass and Don't Come go up to 6×.

Field (one roll)

2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11, 12 win. 3, 4, 9, 10 and 11 pay 1 to 1, the 2 pays 2 to 1 and the 12 pays 3 to 1. 5, 6, 7 and 8 lose.

Propositions

Bonus: All Small · All Tall · Make 'Em All

Put a Bonus bet down any time. It wins if every number in its set rolls after you put it down and before a 7. The tracker on the felt lights up your bet's numbers as they roll. You can add to it or take it down until its first number hits.
